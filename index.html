MBAX 6418 / GROUP FINAL PROJECT

The idea behind ForkCast

An AI-powered restaurant menu assistant, built around individual taste.

The problem we're solving

Dining out should be an enjoyable experience, whether it is lunch with a mentor or dinner with friends. However, choosing what to order can sometimes be overwhelming. Restaurant menus often offer numerous options, making it difficult for customers to decide, especially when they are unfamiliar with certain dishes or ingredients. Many customers also have dietary restrictions, food preferences, religious dietary requirements, budgets, or specific cravings that make selecting a meal more challenging.

Our group proposes developing an AI-powered restaurant menu assistant that simplifies the decision-making process by offering personalized food recommendations based on individual preferences and the restaurant’s available menu.

Our proposed core features

Personalized taste profile
Food preferences, dislikes, dietary restrictions, allergies, religious dietary requirements, spice tolerance, and budget.
Restaurant menu recognition
Upload a photo of a restaurant menu.
Mood-based recommendations
Select a food mood — comforting, hearty, refreshing, light, or adventurous — or describe a craving in your own words.
Feedback and preference learning
Rate an ordered dish and leave written feedback to inform future recommendations.
Mobile interface
Access the website or application from a mobile device.

Professor's feedback

Professor Dobolyi supported the concept and suggested allowing restaurant website or direct menu links in addition to photos. He asked us to decide on the API endpoints before getting too far into development.

The build, so far

This responsive interface uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Python and SQLite are proposed for the future backend. Hermes and Codex are the proposed AI coding tools; GitHub supports version control, documentation, and collaboration. The planned class services are DeepSeek for recommendations and feedback interpretation, and Qwen for menu-photo understanding. They are not connected in this UI preview.
